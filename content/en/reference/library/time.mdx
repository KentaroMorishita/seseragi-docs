## Import

```seseragi
import * as time from "std/time"
```

## Choosing operations

- Instant, local date/time, UTC offset, and time zone are distinct types. Wall-clock display and timeline positions do not implicitly convert.
- Duration is nonnegative, with checked construction and addition. Invalid dates/times are typed errors rather than automatically normalized values.
- Resolving a local date/time in a zone can yield Unique, Ambiguous, or Gap. Applications explicitly choose a policy for daylight-saving overlaps and gaps.
- Timezone data is version-pinned and loading requires the TimeZones capability. Do not assume zone rules vary solely with host configuration.
