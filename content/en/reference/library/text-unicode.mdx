## Choosing operations

- Choose a normalization form explicitly. Canonical NFC/NFD and compatibility NFKC/NFKD equate different sets of sequences.
- String equality compares exact scalar sequences. Visually similar unnormalized sequences are not automatically equal.
- simpleCaseFold maps one scalar to one; fullCaseFold may expand to several scalars. Both are locale-independent.
- Unicode data versions are pinned and checked between toolchain and runtime. Semantics do not depend on host ICU or OS Unicode versions.
